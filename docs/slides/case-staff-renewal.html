Case 2: FROM
EXPIRY TO RENEWAL.

Staff renewals: Beehive tracks every educator's credentials, emails each one a secure upload link before anything expires, and follows up until it's renewed.
TRACK

Spots what's expiring

Watches every WWCC, First Aid, CPR and qualification and flags anything due in the next 60 days.

EMAIL

Emails each educator

Sends a personal email with a secure upload link at 60, 30 and 7 days before the expiry date.

CHASE

Follows up and checks

Sends reminders until the new certificate arrives, then reads it and checks the name and dates.

RENEW

Back on the roster

The owner accepts the new certificate in one tap. Beehive files it, marks the educator current and puts them back on shift.

Beehive does the chasing. The owner makes the call.
